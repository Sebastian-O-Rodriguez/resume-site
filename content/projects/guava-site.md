<p>Guava AI is the company I'm building: practical AI systems for real business problems, starting with distribution and retail operations.</p>

<h2>Why it exists</h2>

<p>I kept noticing the same pattern. Businesses already have the answers they need — the data is there, spread across an ERP, a POS, spreadsheets, and invoices. What's missing is a way to turn it into decisions without weeks of modeling. The thesis became simple: <strong>run your business; we build the systems behind it.</strong></p>

<h2>The interesting problem</h2>

<p>It wasn't the AI. It was trust. An AI that computes your revenue is dangerous; an AI that interprets messy data and hands the arithmetic to deterministic code is useful. That boundary — models for judgment, code for the numbers — became the design spine of everything under the Guava banner.</p>

<h2>Where it's going</h2>

<p>Today I'm building Guava BI, the first product on that spine, and running the company as its founder. The public home for it all lives at guavaai.ai.</p>
